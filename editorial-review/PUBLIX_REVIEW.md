## Proposed draft

Title: Publix attack allegation: what is reported and how mental health courts work

<p>Ramsey Thomas Lyons, 47, is accused of attacking a 70-year-old shopper with a box cutter at a Publix in Punta Gorda, Florida, according to an October 12 report by Law&amp;Crime republished by AOL. That publication described a probable cause affidavit; TheTrueDefender has not independently obtained the affidavit.</p>
<h2>What has been reported</h2>
<p>Law&amp;Crime reported that Lyons faced an aggravated battery charge involving a person aged 65 or older, and that the shopper required hospital treatment. These are reported allegations, not a finding of guilt.</p>
<h2>How the local program is described</h2>
<p>The 20th Judicial Circuit’s 2025 Charlotte County mental health court handbook describes a post-adjudication program: participants enter a plea and receive treatment as a condition of probation. It outlines individualized treatment, case management, court appearances and probation oversight. Eligibility includes legal and clinical assessment.</p>
<p>That handbook describes program rules, not the decision in this defendant’s earlier case. Establishing what happened in that case requires the relevant orders, dates and docket entries. A general description of treatment court cannot establish why supervision ended or whether a particular decision was appropriate.</p>
<h2>National context and remaining questions</h2>
<p>The National Treatment Court Resource Center counted 679 adult mental health courts operating as of December 31, 2024. Its table records the number of programs; it does not measure their outcomes or resolve questions about an individual case.</p>
<p>TheTrueDefender has not independently verified the earlier-case court minutes or the current hearing schedule. Those details, along with the defendant’s representation and any response from prosecutors, need verification before this draft is finalized.</p>
